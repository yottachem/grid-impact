# Panel results

Sample: 326 utility-states, 2015-01 to 2026-07, 43,710 utility-months. Outcome: ln(inflation-adjusted residential price). SEs clustered by utility.

## 1. Local load growth (within balancing authority and month)

Elasticity of real residential price to the utility's own commercial load: **-0.029** (95% CI -0.048 to -0.010, p = 0.003). A 40% rise in commercial load (Dominion-scale) implies -1.0% (CI -1.6% to -0.3%) in real residential price relative to other utilities in the same regional market.

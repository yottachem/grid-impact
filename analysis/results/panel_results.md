# Panel results

Sample: 326 utility-states, 2015-01 to 2026-07, 43,726 utility-months. Outcome: ln(inflation-adjusted residential price). SEs clustered by utility.

## 1. Local load growth (within balancing authority and month)

Elasticity of real residential price to the utility's own commercial load: **-0.026** (95% CI -0.045 to -0.007, p = 0.008). A 40% rise in commercial load (Dominion-scale) implies -0.9% (CI -1.5% to -0.2%) in real residential price relative to other utilities in the same regional market.
